A simple club for students who enjoy basketball and social sport.

Crescent Hoops Club is built around low-pressure basketball activities. Students can come alone, bring friends, join a casual shootaround, and meet new people without needing competitive experience.

The homepage focuses on quick decisions: what the club is, what sessions are available, when they happen, and how a student can join.

What students can do with the club

  • Join weekly basketball sessions

    Each session has a clear activity type, court location, and start time so students can decide quickly.

  • Practise simple skills

    Members can join beginner-friendly drills, shooting practice, and short warmups without needing team experience.

  • Join social games

    Members can join friendly half-court games and suggest future session ideas, making the club feel student-led rather than formal.
